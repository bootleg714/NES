Whoever creates the room is Player 1. Friends who join become Players 2, 3 and 4, and can drop in or out anytime without restarting the game.

For two players on one keyboard, Player 2 uses the number pad (8/4/6/2 to move, 7 = A, 9 = B, 1 = Start, 3 = Select).

Gamepads: plug in up to four for four players on one computer. They become Players 1–4 in the order they're connected. On an Xbox-style pad, the bottom button is B and the right button is A.
